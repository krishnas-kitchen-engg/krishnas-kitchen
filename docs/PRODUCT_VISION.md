# Vision Statement

Build the simplest, fastest, and most volunteer-friendly operational platform for temple and community kitchens.

The platform should reduce:

* operational chaos
* shortages
* overbuying
* wastage
* manual coordination
* inventory inaccuracies
* procurement mistakes
* volunteer onboarding friction

while increasing:

* operational visibility
* inventory accuracy
* preparation reliability
* volunteer efficiency
* feast readiness
* institutional knowledge retention

---

# Mission

Enable volunteer-run kitchens to operate with the reliability and visibility of modern operational systems without the complexity, cost, or training burden of enterprise ERP software.

---

# Core Problem Statement

Temple and community kitchens frequently rely on:

* manual counting
* WhatsApp coordination
* memory-based inventory management
* inconsistent procurement
* unstructured recipes
* volunteer tribal knowledge
* paper notes
* last-minute shortage handling
* duplicate purchasing
* inaccurate stock awareness

This creates:

* operational stress
* feast preparation risk
* food wastage
* inventory inaccuracy
* volunteer confusion
* procurement inefficiency
* preventable emergencies

Existing ERP systems are typically:

* too expensive
* too complex
* warehouse-oriented
* not volunteer-friendly
* difficult to maintain
* difficult to onboard volunteers into
* poorly optimized for feast/event operations

---

# Product Positioning

## Positioning Statement

SevaOps is an operational simplicity platform for volunteer-run kitchens.

Unlike traditional ERP or warehouse systems, SevaOps is designed specifically for:

* rotating volunteers
* feast/event preparation
* high operational variability
* low technical training
* mobile-first workflows
* fast scan-based inventory operations

# Product Principles

## Principle 1 — Operational Simplicity

The system should reduce mental overhead.

Users should not need ERP knowledge.

---

## Principle 2 — Mobile First

All critical workflows must function efficiently on phones.

---

## Principle 3 — Scan First

Barcode/QR scanning should be the primary interaction model wherever possible.

---

## Principle 4 — Volunteer Friendly

Temporary and rotating volunteers should be able to contribute with minimal onboarding.

---

## Principle 5 — Offline Capable

Core operational flows must continue working even with poor internet connectivity.

---

## Principle 6 — Reversible Actions

Mistakes should be easy to undo.

Operational systems must support recovery.

---

## Principle 7 — Auditability

All inventory-changing actions must be traceable.

---

## Principle 8 — Fast Workflows

Most operational actions should complete in under 5 seconds.

---

## Principle 9 — Low Training Requirement

The platform should be intuitive enough for non-technical volunteers.

---

## Principle 10 — Incremental Complexity

Advanced functionality should not overwhelm new users.

---
